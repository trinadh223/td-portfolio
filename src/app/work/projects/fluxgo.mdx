## Context

Fluxgo is solving intercity mobility in India through reliable carpools and shared cabs. I founded it in September 2026 and lead product end to end across iOS, Android, web and a cab partner app. Today it has 140+ members and 50+ rides shared, with an AI engine next focused on ride reliability.

## Problem

Intercity travel in India has a reliability gap. Private buses run long routes, mostly overnight, at fixed times, while solo cabs are costly. Informal carpools offer an alternative, yet they break down on last-minute cancellations, no-shows and unclear pickup. Without visible attendance records, fixed seat prices or verified members, travellers cannot count on a shared ride.

## Users

- Intercity riders seeking safe shared travel, clear seat maps and in-ride safety controls.
- Car owners seeking dependable co-travellers who show up on time.
- Cab partners needing a verified channel to fill intercity seats.

## Key insight

Reliability must sit inside the booking flow, not behind post-ride ratings. Upfront no-show and late-cancel records, fixed seat prices, 1-hour reminders and verified members work together so carpools and shared cabs run as planned.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Restricted rides to verified members with work email badges and vehicle registration checks, because open sign-ups would add supply at the cost of safety.
- Placed each member's no-show and late-cancel record beside ratings on every profile, because visible attendance history gives travellers real context before committing to a seat.
- Added a women co-traveller option, trusted contacts and live location sharing, because safety assurance directly affects whether riders book.
- Required KYC onboarding for cab partners, because adding shared-cab supply only works if partner checks match member trust standards.

## Metrics

- **140+** members and **50+** rides shared
- Live on the App Store, Google Play (Android launched November 2026) and web, plus a cab partner app

## What I learned

- Visible accountability shapes booking confidence more than matching features alone.
- Trust must span the entire journey, from member checks and partner KYC to live trip safety.

## What is next

Next is the Fluxgo AI engine, focused first on ride reliability. AI stays invisible to members, with no chatbot and no AI buttons. It suggests actions while the product decides, rolling out in stages only after it proves accurate.
