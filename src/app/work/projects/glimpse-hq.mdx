## Context

In August 2026, I explored Glimpse HQ as an end-to-end B2B SaaS AI video marketing platform for performance marketing teams. As founder and product lead, I ran structured discovery with a prototype, glimpse-hq.com and brand videos to test the concept before building. I did not launch it.

## Problem

Video marketing for performance teams spans campaign context, signals, diagnosis, hypotheses, ad concepts and scripts, production, launch, results and learning, yet they struggle to keep that loop connected in one workspace. Meanwhile, black-box AI tools hide uncertainty and missing information behind confident summaries, making recommendations hard for practitioners to trust.

## Users

- Performance marketers who need campaign context, diagnosis, video ad concepts, scripts, production, launch and learnings connected in one workflow.
- Marketing leads who need evidence, missing information, confidence, alternatives and approval controls before consequential actions.

## Key insight

Performance marketers will not trust black-box AI recommendations. An AI workspace earns credibility when it fits real team workflows and makes its reasoning and uncertainty inspectable.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Mapped how teams work today before picking features, because grounding AI in real decisions prevents building abstract tools.
- Connected the full loop from context to final learning in one workspace, because isolated tools lose the reasoning behind each decision.
- Required every recommendation to show evidence, flag missing information, state confidence and offer an alternative explanation, because inspectable reasoning beats confident black-box summaries.
- Kept humans in final control with approval for consequential actions, because trust breaks when AI acts without oversight.

## Validation plan

I kept a decision log and an assumption register to turn broad beliefs into concrete questions for practitioner interviews. Instead of static decks, I built a **10**-milestone discovery prototype with a fictional brand backed by real calculations, alongside a live site at glimpse-hq.com, so marketers could inspect real numbers and critique the workflow. Glimpse HQ was explored and not launched.

## What I learned

- Map how a team works today before designing AI so scope stays tied to real decisions.
- Test with a working prototype and real calculations instead of slides to get sharper practitioner feedback.
- Define AI trust rules during discovery to understand what a product demands before committing.
